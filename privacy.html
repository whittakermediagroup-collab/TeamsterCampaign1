Privacy Policy

Teamsters Local 14

This Privacy Policy explains how nomooreretaliation.com (the "Site"), a campaign website operated by the International Brotherhood of Teamsters, Local Union No. 14 ("Local 14," "we," or "us"), handles information when you visit. We have built this Site to share information with the public, not to collect data about you.

Information We Collect

The Site does not ask you to create an account, does not include contact or sign-up forms, and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP addresses and browser type) for security and operational purposes. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Embedded Video Content

This Site displays testimonial videos hosted on YouTube. When you play an embedded video, YouTube and Google may set cookies and collect data about your interaction in accordance with their own privacy policies. This happens directly between your browser and Google; we do not control or receive that data. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies and Tracking

The Site itself does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The only third-party cookies that may be set come from the embedded YouTube videos described above, and only if you interact with them.

How We Use Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade any visitor information. We do not use visitor information for advertising. Any technical logs are used only to keep the Site secure and functioning.

Third-Party Links

The Site links to other websites, including the National Labor Relations Board, Teamsters Local 14's main website, and social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect personal information through the Site, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can control cookies from embedded videos through your browser settings. If you have questions about this policy, you may contact Teamsters Local 14 through the contact information available at teamsters14.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date below.
